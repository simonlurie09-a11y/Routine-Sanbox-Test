# Notes for App Review

Paste into App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes.

```
Thank you for reviewing Companion.

No sign-in is needed: the app has no accounts. Everything is stored on the device.

HEALTHKIT
Companion only reads from HealthKit. It never writes. It asks during sign-up ("Connect Apple Health"), and from Settings → Apple Health. It reads:
- date of birth and sex, to fill in the age step and energy estimates;
- latest height and weight, to fill in the weight step and protein targets;
- sleep, heart rate variability (SDNN) and resting heart rate, to compare last night with the person's own 28-night range on the Today and Recovery tabs.
Nothing read from HealthKit is stored by the app, except the date of birth, height and weight the person keeps in their profile. Nothing is sent off the device, and nothing is used for advertising. If the review device has no Health data, readiness shows "not enough nights yet". Sample sleep, HRV and resting heart rate entries added in the Health app will appear after reopening Companion.

TRY THE MAIN FEATURES
1. Sign up: tick the data box, then Connect Apple Health (or tap Not now), enter a birth year (18 or over), a weight, and a goal.
2. Food tab → Scan a barcode: scan any packaged food. Products come from Open Food Facts (needs a connection). You can also search, quick-add, or add a food from its label.
3. Today → Finish setting up → Health and safety: leave everything off and tap Save. Then Your split → Start from a template → Save.
4. Training → Ask for a change → "A bigger back" → That's right. The app checks its evidence library and suggests a change only when at least 3 verified studies support it. Otherwise it explains why it changed nothing. Each suggestion lists its studies, with links to PubMed or the journal.

SAFETY
The app is for adults (18+) and stops under-18s at sign-up. If the person reports an injury, a medical condition, a pregnancy or a history of disordered eating, the related suggestions are paused. A medical disclaimer is shown at sign-up, with every suggestion, and in Settings → About. The app does not diagnose or treat anything and is not a medical device.

Data: Settings → Your data exports everything (share sheet) or deletes everything.
```
