# Companion privacy policy

*Effective [date you publish it]. Replace the bracketed parts before you publish.*

Companion is an iPhone app for logging food, keeping a training plan and checking recovery. It is made by **[your name or company]** ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: **everything stays on your iPhone, and we don't collect it.**

## What the app stores, and where

Companion keeps what you enter on your iPhone, in the app's own storage, encrypted by iOS with your device passcode:

* your profile: date of birth (or birth year), weight, height, sex for energy equations, your goal, training experience and schedule;
* your answers to the health and safety questions, and any notes you add;
* your food log, the foods you add, and your daily targets;
* your training split, its versions, your workouts, and the suggestions the app made.

We don't have an account system or a server, so none of this is sent to us or to anyone else. It is included in the backups you make of your iPhone (iCloud Backup or a computer), which Apple encrypts.

## Apple Health

If you allow it, Companion **reads** these from Apple Health:

* date of birth and sex;
* your latest height and weight;
* sleep, heart rate variability (HRV) and resting heart rate.

It uses them to fill in your profile, and to compare last night's numbers with your own usual range ("readiness"). Companion:

* never writes anything to Apple Health;
* doesn't save what it reads, except the values you keep in your profile (your date of birth, height and weight); readiness is worked out again from Health each time you open the app;
* never sends Health data to us or to anyone else, and never uses it for advertising, marketing or data mining.

You can change what Companion may read at any time in the Health app (your picture → Apps → Companion), or stop it reading Apple Health in Companion's Settings.

## Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes and pack QR codes while the scanner is open. No photos or video are saved or sent anywhere.

## When the app goes online

* **Open Food Facts.** When you scan a barcode or search online, the app sends the barcode number or your search words to Open Food Facts to look up the product. Nothing about you is sent with it. Like any website, Open Food Facts' servers see your device's IP address. See their privacy policy at https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy.
* **Studies.** When you open a study from the evidence library, it opens in a browser at PubMed, the journal, or doi.org.

Nothing else leaves your iPhone.

## What we don't do

* No analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and no advertising identifier.
* No selling or sharing of your information. We don't have it.
* No AI services. Requests like "a bigger back" are understood on your iPhone with simple rules.

## Your choices

* **See and export:** Settings → Your data → Export your data creates a file with everything the app holds, which you can save or share.
* **Delete:** Settings → Your data → Delete everything erases it all from the app. Deleting the app does the same.
* **Apple Health:** change access in the Health app at any time.

## Children

Companion is for adults, 18 and over. It asks for your birth year and doesn't let anyone younger continue.

## Not medical advice

Companion provides general fitness and nutrition information. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
