# Age rating questionnaire

App Store Connect asks a fixed set of questions; answer them as the app actually is. Check the wording in App Store Connect when you fill it in: Apple revised the questions and ratings (4+, 9+, 13+, 16+ and 18+) in 2025.

| Topic | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Violence, sexual content, nudity, profanity, horror, drugs, alcohol, tobacco, gambling, contests | None | Not in the app. |
| Medical or treatment information | Infrequent / mild | General fitness and nutrition information with a medical disclaimer; no diagnosis or treatment. |
| Health or wellness topics | Yes | Food, training, sleep and heart-rate data. |
| User-generated content, messaging, web browsing | No | No sharing between people. Study links open in a browser view, which doesn't count as unrestricted web access. |
| In-app controls (parental controls, age assurance) | Age assurance: yes | Sign-up asks for the birth year and stops anyone under 18. |

**Recommended rating: 18+.** The app is built for adults and stops anyone younger at sign-up. If App Store Connect lets you raise the calculated rating, choose 18+ so the store matches the app.
